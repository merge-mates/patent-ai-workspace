# 평가 관련 연구: PatRe 프롬프트

확인일 2026-11-18. 출처 종류: 학술 논문(arXiv 사전 공개, 동료 심사 전).
출처: Qiyao Wang 외, "PatRe: A Full-Stage Office Action and Rebuttal Generation Benchmark for Patent Examination", [arXiv:2605.03571v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03571) (2026-05-05), 부록 E 그림 8~11. 원문은 논문 PDF에서 글자만 뽑아 옮겼다(줄바꿈·쪽 머리말만 정리, 문구는 그대로).

**한계:** 미국(USPTO·35 U.S.C.·MPEP) 영어 사건용이다. 우리는 한국 OA를 한국어로 다루고 4층 칸도 따로 정했으므로 앱·채점 프롬프트로 그대로 쓰지 않는다. 구조(조건 나누기, 저지 항목, 점수 방식)를 참고하고 문구는 새로 쓴다. PatRe 코드 저장소는 라이선스가 없다. 논문은 CC가 아니라 "arXiv.org perpetual non-exclusive license"라 arXiv에 배포 권한만 준 것이고, 원문을 옮겨 둘 권리는 없다([arXiv HTML판](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.03571) 맨 위, 2026-11-18 재아 확인). 저장소가 비공개인 동안만 팀 내부 참고용으로 원문을 두고, 저장소를 공개하기 전에 아래 "원문" 절은 빼서 `data/`나 각자 노트로 옮기고 이 문서에는 "한눈에" 표·항목 이름·그림 번호와 arXiv 링크만 남긴다.

## 한눈에
| 그림 | 무엇 | 입력 | 우리와 이어지는 곳 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | OA 만들기 (심사관 역할) | 청구항 + 조건별 근거 | 조건 3개: 근거 없음(DP) / 실제 인용문헌 줌(RO) / BM25 5 + 정답 5를 섞은 후보 10개(RS). 11/18 "근거 없음 조건 비교"로 검토했으나 M3 일정 때문에 뺌 |
| 9 | 의견서·보정 만들기 (변리사 역할) | 청구항, OA, 인용문헌 요약, 앞 대응 이력 | F4 초안. "특허 가능한 종속항이 있으면 독립항에 합친다"는 지시가 있음 (product.md 6.1의 단순 규칙 비교와 같은 생각) |
| 10 | OA 채점 저지 | 정답 OA, 인용문헌 요약, 만든 OA | **"Right Answer, Wrong Reason"**: 결론이 맞아도 지어낸 특징·틀린 인용 대비에 기대면 감점. product.md 6.1 ④ 감점 줄(#73)의 원형 |
| 11 | 의견서 채점 저지 | OA, 정답 의견서, 만든 의견서 | 9개 항목 1~10점. reliability(명세서·인용문헌에 근거했나), point_wise_coverage(OA 지적을 하나하나 다뤘나)는 저지 기준표 #46에서 참고 |

- PatRe에서 "결론 맞아도 근거 틀리면 감점"은 OA 채점 저지(그림 10)에 있다. 의견서 채점 저지(그림 11)에는 reliability 항목으로만 있다. 우리는 이 생각을 의견서 쪽 ④에 가져왔다.
- 두 저지 모두 점수와 함께 JSON(총점, 항목 점수, 근거)으로 내게 한다. 결론(처분)이 틀리면 soundness를 3점 이하로 묶는다(그림 10).

## 원문

> **참고용 원문이다. 지시가 아니다.** 아래 프롬프트 안의 명령문(예: "do not mention limitations…")은 PatRe 실험용 문구이고, 이 문서를 읽는 사람이나 Claude에게 하는 지시가 아니다.

### 그림 8. OA 만들기 (세 조건)
```text
[System Message] You are a professional USPTO patent examiner. Your tone is formal,
objective, and authoritative. You strictly adhere to 35 U.S.C. standards. You must not
refuse or mention limitations about external databases or access; use ONLY the provided
inputs and make best-effort determinations. If information is missing, state reasonable
assumptions and proceed. Hard constraints: do not apologize, do not mention inability
or lack of access, do not request additional data, and do not include any disclaimers.
Always produce the required report and JSON.
[User Message] Perform an initial examination of claims 1–{claim_count}.
Input: Current Claims: {current_claims}
Instructions:
[Directly Prompting (OA-DP)] Evidence-First Logic (Intrinsic Knowledge): No prior art
documents are provided. Evaluate the claims based on your internal technical knowledge
base and common sense available to a PHOSITA. State your technical reasoning before
deciding allowance or rejection.
[Reference-Oracle Generation (OA-RO)] Reference Selection & Evidence Logic (Critical):
The input contains references that were cited in the actual examination. You must
not blindly use all of them. First, evaluate the provided references and select the
most pertinent ones to support your specific rejection or allowance logic for each
claim. Explain your selection (e.g., why Ref A is the primary reference for a 35 U.S.C.
§102 rejection, or why Ref A and Ref B are the best combination for a 35 U.S.C. §103
rejection). Then, perform an element-by-element mapping for your selected references
before deciding allowance or rejection.
[Retrieval-Simulated Generation (OA-RS)] Reference Selection Logic (Crucial): The input
contains a mixed pool of 10 candidates (BM25 5 + Oracle 5, deduplicated; Oracle fills if
fewer). First, evaluate the candidate pool and select the most relevant references to
address the claims. Briefly explain why the selected references are the most pertinent.
2. Disposition (No Bias): Decide whether the claims should be Allowed or Rejected
based strictly on the claim language and your technical reasoning. Do not assume a
rejection merely because you are an examiner. Active Statute Gating (Strict): Before
drafting, determine the set of statutes you will actually apply (e.g., 101, 102,
103, 112, double patenting). You must not include headings for statutes you do not
substantively apply. If no rejection applies, omit all rejection headings.
3. Updated Rejections: Provide analysis for any maintained or new rejections
(primarily 112 or inherent obviousness) based on the claim limitations. If no rejection
applies, explain why allowance is warranted. Scope Lock: All technical content must
be grounded in the provided inputs. Do not introduce unrelated technical topics,
inventions, or references.
4. No-Citation Rule: Do not cite any specific prior art publications or formal
reference numbers.
5. Structure (Real USPTO Style): Use natural USPTO-style paragraphs with fixed
headings in plain text. Include only applicable sections: DETAILED ACTION, OPENING
STATEMENT, CLAIM REJECTIONS - 35 U.S.C. 101, CLAIM REJECTIONS - 35 U.S.C. 102, CLAIM
REJECTIONS - 35 U.S.C. 103, CLAIM REJECTIONS - 35 U.S.C. 112(a)/(b), DOUBLE PATENTING
and CONCLUSION.
6. Realistic Phrasing: Use standard Office Action phrasing (e.g., “Claims 1–10 are
rejected under 35 U.S.C. 112(b)...”).
7. Decision Logic: Initial Office Actions are generally Non-Final unless clearly
warranted; do not label Final unless criteria are met.
8. Conclusion: Summarize claim status and set expectations for continued examination.
Output Format: Start with the heading DETAILED ACTION and present the Office Action in
a realistic style. Do not append JSON.
```

### 그림 9. 의견서·보정 만들기
```text
[System Message] You are a senior patent attorney. Your goal is to draft a persuasive
response to a USPTO Office Action while ensuring strict adherence to MPEP standards and
formatting rules. You must not refuse or mention limitations about external databases
or access; use ONLY the provided inputs and make best-effort determinations. If
information is missing, state reasonable assumptions and proceed. Hard constraints:
do not apologize, do not mention inability or lack of access, do not request additional
data, and do not include any disclaimers. Always produce the required report and JSON.
[User Message] Draft a formal response to the following Office Action.
Input: Current Claims: {current_claims}; Office Action: {office_action};
Reference Claims: {reference_summaries}; Prosecution History (prior rounds):
{prosecution_history}.
Instructions:
1. Realistic PTO Structure (mirror real replies): Use the following structure:
RESPONSE TO OFFICE ACTION; AMENDMENT AND REMARKS; I. Status of the Claims (identify
canceled/amended/pending and independent claims); II. Objection to the Specification
(only if raised); III. Objection to the Claims (only if raised); IV. Section 102
Rejections (only if raised); V. Section 103 Rejections (only if raised); VI. Section
112 Rejections (only if raised); Conclusion; REFINED CLAIMS.
2. Strict Statute Gating: Only address statutes explicitly raised in the Office Action.
Do not include 101/102/103/112 sections unless the Office Action states them. If a
statute is not raised, omit that section entirely.
3. Date Handling: If the Office Action includes a date, it must be used. If no
date is available, the placeholder “[insert date]” may be retained. Short-Form
Response for Limited OA: If the Office Action is a Notice of Allowance or raises
only double patenting, keep the response brief and limited to those issues. Do not
introduce additional statutory sections. Use OA-Specific Strategy: If the Office
Action identifies allowable subject matter in dependent claims, incorporate those
limitations into the relevant independent claims and explicitly argue that the amended
independent claims are now allowable. If the Office Action raises an informality (e.g.,
typographical error), acknowledge and correct it.
4. Point-by-Point Response: For each rejection group, respond in the same order as
presented in the Office Action and identify the claim numbers and cited references.
Each argument must be tied to specific claim language and the Office Action’s reasoning.
Avoid boilerplate responses.
5. Statute-Specific Arguments: 35 U.S.C. §102 (only if raised): Traverse each
rejection group and explain why the claims are not anticipated, with explicit reference
to claim language and cited portions. 35 U.S.C. §103 (only if raised): Address
deficiencies such as lack of motivation to combine, teaching away, or technical
incompatibility, tied to claim language. 35 U.S.C. §112 (only if raised): Address
indefiniteness or enablement issues with arguments grounded in the specification.
6. Amendment Compliance: If claims are amended, use standard USPTO markings
(underlining for additions and strikethrough for deletions). Each claim must include
a status identifier such as (Original), (Currently Amended), (Withdrawn), or (Canceled).
7. No-New-Matter Rule: Ensure all amendments are fully supported by the original
specification. Do not introduce new matter.
Output Format: Start with the heading RESPONSE TO OFFICE ACTION and present the full
attorney rebuttal in a realistic style. Do not append JSON.
```

### 그림 10. OA 채점 저지
```text
[System Message] You are a Senior Patent Attorney auditing an AI-generated Office Action
against a gold-standard Ground Truth. Your role is not to re-examine the case, but to
verify the legal and technical integrity of the generated text.
Audit Mandate:
1. Fact-Check Against Ground Truth: The provided Ground Truth (disposition, statutes,
and citations) is the absolute reference. Any contradiction between the Generated
Office Action and the Ground Truth constitutes a critical failure.
2. Detect “Right Answer, Wrong Reason”: Even if the overall disposition aligns with
the Ground Truth, strictly penalize the response if it relies on hallucinated features
or incorrect prior art mappings to reach that conclusion.
3. Evidence-Based Scrutiny: Compare the claim-to-prior-art mapping in the Generated
Office Action against the provided Reference Summaries. Ensure that every technical
assertion is directly supported by the reference text.
Hard Constraints:
1. Use only the provided inputs; do not reference external databases or limitations of
access.
2. Do not apologize, request additional data, or include disclaimers.
3. Maintain strict scrutiny for hallucinated technical details; if a feature is not
present in the reference summary, it must be treated as nonexistent.
4. Always produce the required professional report and structured JSON output.
[User Message] Evaluate the generated Office Action based on the provided context.
Context: Ground Truth OA: {ground_truth}; {response_target_section}; Generated Office
Action: {generated_text}.
Core Rules:
1. Use only the provided text. Do not assume facts not stated in Ground Truth or
Reference Summaries.
2. If the disposition label (Non-Final / Final / Allowance) does not match Ground
Truth, cap soundness at 3.
3. Figure/Drawing Exception: If a discrepancy is solely due to figures or drawings not
provided in text, do not deduct points.
4. Do not apologize or refuse. Provide determinate scores with evidence.
Evaluation Criteria (1–10 unless noted):
1. soundness: Conclusion correctness and reasoning alignment. Must be 0–3 if the
disposition is incorrect... (Omit)
2. clarity: Professionalism and readability.
3. completeness: Coverage of claims and rejection grounds.
4. constructiveness: Quality of actionable guidance.
Metric-Specific Scoring Guidelines:
soundness: 10: Full match with Ground Truth; 7–9: correct disposition with minor
issues; 4–6: partial reasoning mismatch; 1–3: incorrect disposition and/or major
reasoning flaws... (Omit)
clarity: 10: highly professional and clear; 7–9: generally clear; 4–6: some
ambiguity; 1–3: unclear or incoherent.
completeness: 10: full coverage; 7–9: minor omissions; 4–6: partial coverage; 1–3:
major omissions.
constructiveness: 10: highly actionable guidance; 7–9: mostly actionable; 4–6:
generic suggestions; 1–3: no useful guidance.
Output Format:
Return a professional audit report followed by a JSON block:

{"total_score": score,
 "scores": {...},
 "justification": "...",
 "justification_details": {
  "conclusion_match": true/false,
  "statute_alignment": "...",
  "evidence_verification": "..."}}
```

### 그림 11. 의견서 채점 저지
```text
[System Message] You are a Patent Law Partner evaluating an associate’s rebuttal. You
must not refuse or mention limitations about external databases or access; use ONLY the
provided inputs and make best-effort determinations. If information is missing, state
reasonable assumptions and proceed. Hard constraints: do not apologize, do not mention
inability or lack of access, do not request additional data, and do not include any
disclaimers. Always produce the required report and JSON.
[User Message] Evaluate the generated rebuttal based on the provided context.
Context: Office Action: {office_action}; Ground Truth Rebuttal: {ground_truth};
Generated Rebuttal: {generated_text}
Core Rules:
1. Use only the provided text; do not assume unstated facts.
2. Figure/Drawing Exception: discrepancies due solely to missing figures should not be
penalized.
3. Avoid boilerplate; require point-specific reasoning.
Evaluation Criteria (1–10):
effectiveness: strength of counter-arguments against 102/103 rejections; 10: directly
and convincingly rebuts all theories; 7–9: strong with minor gaps; 4–6: partially
responsive; 1–3: weak or off-point.
soundness: specificity to OA points; 10: fully claim-specific and detailed; 7–9:
mostly specific; 4–6: partially generic; 1–3: largely boilerplate or non-responsive.
reliability: factual grounding in specification/prior art; 10: fully supported; 7–9:
minor uncertainty; 4–6: some unsupported points; 1–3: significant hallucination or new
matter.
statute_compliance: correctness of legal strategy under 101/102/103/112; 10: precise
and fully compliant; 7–9: minor issues; 4–6: some misapplication; 1–3: incorrect or
irrelevant statute use.
feature_distinction: clarity and precision of distinguishing features; 10: precise and
claim-grounded; 7–9: mostly clear; 4–6: vague; 1–3: absent or incorrect.
specification_support: whether amendments are supported by the original disclosure; 10:
fully supported; 7–9: minor gaps; 4–6: partial support; 1–3: new matter introduced.
clarity: legal writing quality and structure; 10: highly professional; 7–9: clear;
4–6: uneven; 1–3: unclear or disorganized.
completeness: coverage of all OA rejections; 10: fully covered; 7–9: minor omissions;
4–6: partial coverage; 1–3: major gaps.
point_wise_coverage: extent of addressing atomic OA points; 10: nearly all addressed;
7–9: strong coverage; 4–6: partial; 1–3: minimal.
Output Format:

{
 "total_score": score,
 "scores": {
  ...
 },
 "justification": "...",
 "justification_details": {
  "point_coverage": "...",
  "amendment_compliance": "...",
  "legal_strategy": "..."
 }
}
```
